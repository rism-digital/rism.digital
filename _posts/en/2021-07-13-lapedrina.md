The ensemble “La Pedrina”, conducted by Francesco Saverio Pedrini, will once again perform in concert the programme from the last CD produced by RISM Switzerland: [“Il Pastor fido”](/rism-ch/cds/cd-marenzio.html), with madrigals by Luca Marenzio from a source preserved in Zuoz. The concert, already programmed for October 2020 and cancelled due to the Covid, will take place on Friday 13 August as part of the prestigious [Engadin festival](http://www.engadinfestival.ch//la-pedrina-277).
The CD recording was released in 2017 for the label [Claves Records](https://www.claves.ch/). The programme was performed in Zurich and Zuoz in 2017 and in Bellinzona in 2020. After three years there is again the possibility to listen to this music at the place where it is kept, in the church of San Luzi.
