At the middle of February 2018, RISM Switzerland begins a three-year collaboration with the Cantonal and University Library of Fribourg. The project aims to catalogue the BCU's historical music collection. This contains a large amount of music sources, which have been until now hardly, if at all, examined. There are two different collections: on the one hand the manuscripts from the Monastery of Montorge and on the other a large collection summarised under the group signature "EBAZ".

See the [project page](/cataloging/bcu-fribourg.html) for more information about the project.
