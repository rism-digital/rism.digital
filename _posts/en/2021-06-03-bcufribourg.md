Immerse yourself in the rich musical world of the Capuchin nuns of Montorge from the end of the 18th to the beginning of the 19th century. The lecture on the musical life of the nuns is followed by a concert of sacred music for 4 voices and organ with compositions from the Montorge musical collection deposited at Fribourg University Library. 
The video of the event is available on the [YouTube channel of the Fribourg University Library](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aToAs3P-EFc).
