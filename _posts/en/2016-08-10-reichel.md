RISM Switzerland starts in late July 2016 to catalog the collection of the Bernese composer Adolf Reichel (1816-1896) that was considered lost until 2013. The collection was donated to the Music Library of the University of the Arts in Bern by his great-grandson Helmuth Reichel.

The cataloging of the Adolf Reichel collection is part of the [project Repertory of 19th-century Swiss composers](http://rism-ch.org/cataloging/repertorium.html?locale=en) that aims to fill gaps in the field of Swiss music documentation.

See the [project page](/rism-ch/cataloging/reichel.html) for more information about the project.
