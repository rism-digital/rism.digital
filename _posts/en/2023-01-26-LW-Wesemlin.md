Since the summer of 2022, the Swiss RISM Centre has been cataloguing the music collection from the [Provincial Archives of the Swiss Capuchins](https://www.klosterluzern.ch/provinzarchiv/) in the Wesemlin Monastery in Lucerne. This is the first time that the holdings of a Capuchin monastery have been systematically catalogued. Although the music tradition of the Minorite friars does not go back very far, due to the long-lasting rejection of church music, the holdings, almost exclusively from the 20th century, are very extensive. They contain primarily liturgical music for daily prayers and mass celebrations. In the [RISM-Online](https://rism.online/search?q=CH-Lw&mode=sources&fq=sigla%3ACH-Lw&fb=sigla%3Aintersection&page=1&rows=20), the easiest way to find the sources that have already been indexed is to use the siglum search (CH-Lw).
