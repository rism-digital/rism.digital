# OnStage: HEMU -- Lausanne conservatory

At the end of 2012, the Swiss RISM inaugurated a collaboration with the
School of Music (HEMU) - Lausanne Conservatory for the OnStage project
concerning historical concert programmes. This project was funded by the
HEMU itself. Its goal was the indexing and digitization of concert
programmes kept in the Conservatoire library. The model proposed by RISM
Switzerland for this project was the TEI Project \"Kapellmeisterbuch\".
Using the TEI XML standard gives the project a high scientific profile
and ensures good interoperability for possible future developments. RISM
participated in this project on two levels, namely by scanning sources
and by developing an online publication platform. The concert programmes
were commented with texts written by employees of the Conservatory and
were made available online at the end of September 2013.

The project was presented at a conference in late November 2013 at the
Lausanne Conservatory (P. Boschetti, V. Struijk Van Bergen and L.
Pugin).
