# OnStage: Freunde Alter Musik Basel

The present project adds a third collection to the concert program
database [On Stage] (in the series [RISM digital library]), after the
collections of the "Haute Ecole de Musique de Lausanne -- Conservatoire
de Lausanne (HEMU-CL)" and the "Conservatoire de Musique de Genève". The
association "Freunde Alter Musik Basel" (FAMB) was founded in 1942 by
Paul Sacher and his colleagues. The programs and the names of the
performers provide a cultural and historical overview of the development
of the early music movement. The programs of the first 50 years of FAMB
concerts were listed in 1992 in a paper publication (Alte Musik --
Konzert und Rezeption, ed. by Veronika Gutmann, Winterthur 1992, pp.
234-352). The online publication of the original documents since the
foundation of the FAMB is a contribution to the 75th anniversary of the
association in 2017 and will be updated regularly. The publication is a
collaborative project between RISM Switzerland and the [Association
FAMB].

First, the entire collection is digitized, whereupon the data is indexed
and the text is automatically read using OCR technology to enable a
full-text search. The homepage guarantees both a clear presentation of
the different collections as well as optimal search functionality over
the entire corpus

The project will be terminated a the end of 2016.
