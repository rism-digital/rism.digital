### Music collections in Swiss archives and libraries

The lists [Collections -- People] and [Collections -- Institutions] give
an overview of collections with an emphasis on music held in Swiss
archives and libraries. They contain autographs, manuscripts, prints,
material linked to works, sound recordings, handwritten texts, printed
texts, correspondence, photographs and other personal or business
documents.
