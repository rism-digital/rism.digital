# Publications

New knowledge frequently arises alongside the cataloguing work or is
inspired by it. The staff of the RISM Digital Center publish such insights in
books or scientific articles.

In addition, the RISM Digital Center is itself active as a publisher of online
publication series (e.g., RISM Digital Library and Historical Music
Inventories). These publications give the public access to archival
sources (i.e., sources without music notation) offering important
insights into the musical life of Switzerland and beyond.
